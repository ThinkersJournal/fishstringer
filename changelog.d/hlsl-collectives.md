---
Board 506. New public `Slang::lower_kernel_hlsl` and `Slang::lower_module_hlsl` lower a `Subgroup`-scope `Collective` to HLSL wave intrinsics: `Reduce` Sum/Prod/Max/Min/And/Or/Xor over `I32`/`U32`/`F32` (a float Max/Min declines by name: the HLSL NaN rule is unmeasured), `Shuffle` Idx/Up/Down/Xor (a lane with no source keeps its own value, as the oracle does), `Vote` All/Any (a `U32` 0/1) and `Broadcast`. The default `lower_kernel`/`lower_module` are unchanged and still decline every collective; `Scan`, `ReduceWith`/`ScanWith`, `CollectiveTuple`, `Vote` Ballot and block scope stay declined on both. Each collective is wrapped in `__target_switch { case hlsl: .. }` with no default, so `slangc` fails the text for SPIR-V, GLSL, Metal, WGSL and CUDA: the SPIR-V and GLSL declines are not lifted by the back door. Measured with slangc 2026.8: HLSL compiles, the other five targets fail, and an unguarded control compiles for all six. Test `lower_kernel_hlsl_collectives`: all 14 lowered forms compile for HLSL and are refused for SPIR-V and GLSL, a guard-removed mutant compiles for SPIR-V, the declines are named. Not run on a device or with dxc: the HLSL text is compiled by slangc only, and WaveReadLaneAt with a per-lane (non-uniform) lane argument in Up/Down/Xor is UNVERIFIED against DXC's shader-model rules. The patent question (US8539204B2) stays open: docs/PATENT_SWEEP.md. Proposed 0.9.0: new public functions are additive, but a decline becomes a lowering on them, and a collective-using kernel's behaviour on the HLSL path is new (pm-assist rules).
