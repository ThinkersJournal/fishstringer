---
The root `LICENSE-APACHE` and `fishstringer-kernels-source/LICENSE-APACHE` differed from the canonical Apache-2.0 text (section 6 lacked "reasonable and customary use in", section 9 was reworded, among other differences) and from the copies in `fishstringer-slang-emit` and `fishstringer-slang-parse`. Both now carry those crates' copy, whose words match the text shipped in serde's `LICENSE-APACHE` (compared word by word; serde's copy omits the appendix, ours keeps it). `slang_binary_int` masks a shift count with 63 for `U64` as it already did for `I64` (it would have used 31 had `U64` ever reached it; today `U64` binary ops are declined before it); covered by a unit assertion.
