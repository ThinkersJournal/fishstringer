kind: Slang entry-model reader (`SLANG.entry_model`) and `Backend::lower_with` / `CallerCaps` for runtime `Param`s; unpopped family 0.56.12
---
`fishstringer-slang-parse` gains `read_entry_model`, a CST reader wired into `SLANG` through `Frontend::with_entry_model`: the `[numthreads]` block, the `SV_DispatchThreadID` / `SV_GroupID` / `SV_GroupThreadID` / `SV_GroupIndex` semantics, and the file-scope `StructuredBuffer` / `RWStructuredBuffer` / `ConstantBuffer` globals the entry references, in slot order with the constant buffer last. A Slang entry whose resources are globals now lifts through `lift_kernel`; the six reference cases of unpopped-convert's `kernel_body_entry_model.rs` pass against this reader (`tests/entry_model.rs`), plus unreferenced-resource, constant-buffer-last and named-decline cases. `fishstringer-slang-emit` overrides `Backend::lower_with`: a plan that reads a runtime scalar `Param` is refused with `UnsupportedPlanShape` for a `CallerCaps::LEGACY` caller (`lower` and `try_generate` are that caller), and lowered for `CallerCaps::with_interface()` to a `ConstantBuffer` after the buffers with explicit `[[vk::binding]]` slots and a `KernelInterface` (bindings, params block, params); parameterless plans are byte-identical. The unpopped family moves 0.56.1 to 0.56.12 (crates.io). slangc compiles the new kernel; NOT device-run. Still open: tree-sitter-slang 0.3.1 puts `[shader("compute")]` in an ERROR node (the reader tolerates it).
